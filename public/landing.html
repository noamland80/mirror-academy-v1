In sixty seconds

A client decides whether you are safe to be honest with. That decision — not your treatment menu — decides the outcome.

She will not say it out loud. She will say “I’ll think about it”, leave, and book somewhere else or nowhere. MIRROR trains the twenty minutes in which that decision is actually made.

The problem

She is deciding something else

While your practitioner describes the treatment, the client is answering a different question: can I tell this person the real reason I am here? When the answer is no she keeps it, the recommendation is aimed at the wrong thing, and the consultation ends politely and empty.

What your team does

Sixty interactive lessons, then whole consultations with nine clients who react. Every reply your practitioner chooses changes what the client discloses next, which objection arrives, and whether a yes is still reachable. The ending is derived from what she did — nobody selects it.
